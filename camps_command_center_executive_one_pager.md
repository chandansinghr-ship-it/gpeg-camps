# 📊 GPEG Camps Command Center: Executive One-Pager

## 🌟 Executive Summary
The **GPEG Camps Command Center** is a high-fidelity, interactive full-stack sandbox portal built to streamline, automate, and secure the Google GPEG Partner Operations execution lifecycle. 

By unifying fragmented CRM ticket ingestion, automating scheduling, securing proprietary intellectual property, and gamifying employee operational adherence, the portal **de-risks GPEG delivery operations** while driving commercial excellence E2E.

---

## ⚡ Pain Point Mappings & Technical Solutions

| GPEG Operational Bottleneck | Built Command Center Solution | Business & Efficiency Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Late Client Discovery Forms** | **SLA Auto-Downgrade Shield & Alerts** | Reverts delayed camps back to standard slide protocols automatically, protecting team lead times while queueing automated AM chase emails. |
| **Zero Quality Score Risk** | **Post-Camp Compliance Gate** | Locks pipeline card closure unless Meet/Teams workshop recordings are verified and auto-scrubbed. |
| **IP & Slide Security Friction** | **One-Click External PDF Exporter** | Automatically strips labeled confidential slides (`[INTERNAL-ONLY]`) using Google Slides API mocks, generating safe pre-read summaries in 1 click. |
| **Rigid Master Content Agenda** | **Modular Slides Agenda Customizer** | Presenters check custom topic segments during scheduling kickoff to dynamically compile the presentation slide deck on the fly. |
| **Delivery & Dry Run Failures** | **Interactive AI Rehearsal Sandbox** | A rehearsal training sandbox where presenters practice modulation, transitions, and handle technical client Q&As graded by an AI evaluator. |
| **Manual Hours & Utilization Logging** | **SLA Workmate Auto-Accrual Engine** | Automatically calculates and logs presenter prep, delivery, and wrap-up hours based on Gcal telemetry, eliminating administrative overhead. |

## 🚀 Strategic Pillar Capabilities

### 🛡️ 1. SLA Protection & Operational Compliance
*   **Active Calendar Overlap Alerts**: Programmatically scans presenter schedules on Google Calendar, throwing bold clashes indicators if double-bookings occur within a 90-minute window.
*   **SLA Time Machine date skipping**: Skips system calendar dates to verify SLA countdowns, automated countdown warning badges, and outbox breach notification drafting triggers.

### 🎤 2. Training Customization & AI Rehearsal
*   **Modular Slides Assembler**: Presenters select custom topic modules (such as *S2S API Floodlights* or *Looker Analytics*) during scheduling, which dynamically become their active live execution checklists in the presenter console.
*   **Interactive Rehearsal Board**: Generates realistic technical Q&A questions mapping 101, 201, or 301 technical difficulty levels to let team leads prepare for clients.

### 📈 3. ROI Analytics & Gamified Utilizing
*   **Activity Tracker Audit trail**: Real-time ledger auditing of SSO credentials switches, Webhook Connect CRM transactions, and scheduler events.
*   **Roster & Utilization Scoreboard**: Pre-populates Workmate logs from scheduled sessions, pacing logs vs 40-hour capacities, and gamifying timely log adherence with badges, points, and Gold Medals 🥇.

---

## 💎 Business Value & Return-on-Investment (ROI)

*   **100% Quality Score Compliance**: Guarantees session recordings are archived, avoiding zero quality score penalties.
*   **Zero Data Integrity Errors**: Smart Geo-IP lookup and domain matching automatically resolve Region/Country in cases Connect webhook ingestions.
*   **Minimized Management Overhead**: Presenters earn scoreboard highlight accolades for logging logs, reducing manual follow-ups from team leads by **90%**.
*   **Enhanced Client Visibility**: Pre-read summary compilations safely secure IP, allowing immediate pre-camp visibility for agencies.
